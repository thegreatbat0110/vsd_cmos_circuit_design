Velocity saturation
-------------------- 

It is a non-ideal effect in short-channel MOSFETs where charge carriers (electrons or holes) stop accelerating as the electric field increases. It causes the drain current to scale linearly rather than quadratically with gate voltage and limits the maximum achievable switching speed of the transistor.
